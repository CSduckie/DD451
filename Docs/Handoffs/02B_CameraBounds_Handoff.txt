Validation / lifecycle
- Requires same loaded scene for Binder, followCamera and native BoundingVolume.
- Bounds must be BoxCollider, component enabled, own GameObject activeSelf, isTrigger=true.
- No attached Rigidbody, Renderer on bounds object, or Interactable in its ancestry.
- Bounds cannot be under any Camera (including OutputCamera), CinemachineCamera, player,
  or player's cameraTarget. Ancestor component checks include dormant ancestors.
- Inactive ancestors during additive staging are permitted by ValidateBounds. They are not
  equated with disabling the BoxCollider or its own GameObject.
- Bind may be called during staging. Native Follow is kept null until Binder, camera and
  bounds are active in hierarchy. Update then completes binding. This avoids a following
  frame with an inactive volume; it does not change scene activation itself.
- Bind, target replacement, native-volume reference replacement, unbind and teleport reset
  PreviousStateIsValid. Teleport also retains OnTargetObjectWarped(Follow, delta).
- Runtime Inspector edits to the same Box center/size or its world transform are supported:
  Binder keeps noneditable geometry snapshots and invalidates old slowing/damping history on
  change. It syncs physics transforms only on initial/rebinding, geometry changes and teleport,
  before the native extension's next ClosestPoint sample, not every ordinary follow frame.
- Runtime snapshots boundPlayer/boundCamera/boundVolume are not editable configuration.
  Switching followCamera clears Follow and state on the old camera as well as initializing
  the new one. Old player Teleported subscriptions are removed.
- OnDisable/Bind(null) release Follow and runtime snapshots but retain THIS scene's authored
  native BoundingVolume so ordinary deactivate/reactivate or rollback can bind again.
- Null, disabled or invalid volumes clear following and report [PlayerCameraBounds] once per
  continuous failure (or explicit Bind attempt). A foreign-scene BoundingVolume is additionally
  cleared from the native extension so it cannot keep using another map. Assign the correct
  local volume to recover; disabled same-scene configuration can recover when re-enabled.
- Missing player with the shared prefab's player=null does not produce a null-bounds warning.
- DefaultExecutionOrder(-100) runs validity checks in Update before the usual Brain LateUpdate.
  Integration must still configure/activate bounds before presenting the new camera. Do not
  mutate scene camera references after its Brain update and expect same-frame ordering magic.
